I had the incredible opportunity to attend the **[ACM Asian School on HPC and AI 2026](https://europe.acm.org/seasonal-schools/asean/2026)**. The program brought together students, researchers, and professionals from across Asia and beyond to explore the latest advances in **High Performance Computing (HPC)** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

This experience was not only academically enriching but also culturally inspiring.

## 🗻 Visiting a World-Class Supercomputer Facility

One of the highlights of the school was visiting a state-of-the-art supercomputing Fugaku center. Seeing the computing racks in person made everything we learned feel tangible and real.

Standing next to the system reminded me that behind every large AI model and scientific simulation lies massive engineering effort and computational power. This visit deepened my appreciation for the infrastructure that powers modern AI research.

## 🗾 Cultural Exchange & Memories in Japan

Exploring the city together was another unforgettable part of the journey. Walking through vibrant streets at night, enjoying local food, and sharing laughter with new friends made the experience even more meaningful.

Science connects us globally, but shared experiences make those connections lasting.
