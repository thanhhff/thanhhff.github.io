I had the incredible opportunity to attend **SCA/HPCAsia 2026 (Supercomputing Asia 2026)**, held in Osaka, Japan. The conference brought together researchers, engineers, and industry leaders working at the forefront of **High-Performance Computing (HPC), AI, Cloud, and Quantum Computing**.

This event was not only technically inspiring but also a valuable chance to connect with the global HPC community.

## Quantum Computing: IBM Quantum System Two

Another impressive exhibit was the **IBM Quantum System Two**, showcasing the integration of quantum computing with HPC systems. The hybrid future of classical HPC and quantum computing is becoming increasingly tangible — and SCA 2026 made that vision feel closer than ever.

## Networking & Community

Beyond the technical sessions, SCA provided a valuable space to connect with fellow researchers and industry professionals. Conversations ranged from GPU architectures to distributed training strategies and future collaborations.

Moments like these remind me that progress in computing is powered not only by machines — but by people.
