I was excited to be back at **[IEEE/CVF WACV 2026](https://wacv2026.thecvf.com/)** this year, held in **Tucson, Arizona** (March 6–10, 2026). After a great experience at WACV 2025, returning this year was especially meaningful — I presented 2 papers at the poster sessions:

- **ViCoKD: View-aware Cross-modal Distillation for Multi-view Action Recognition**
- **PADM: A Physics-aware Diffusion Model for Attenuation Correction**
