I attended the **[22nd IEEE International Conference on Advanced Video and Signal-Based Surveillance (AVSS 2026)](https://www.avss2026.org/)**, held in **Lecce, Italy**, under the theme *"Beyond Monitoring"*. The conference opened at the Convitto Palmieri and continued in the beautiful setting of the **Carlo V Castle** — a memorable venue for a few days of talks on video analysis, surveillance, and signal-based systems.

---

## 🎤 TraRA at AVSS 2026

Our paper, **"TraRA: Trajectory-level Recognition Aggregation for Video Text Spotting in Urban Surveillance"**, was presented by **Duc Tri Tran**, the first author of the work. Tri gave a great talk introducing the motivation behind trajectory-level aggregation for reading text in urban surveillance video, where individual frames are often blurry, occluded, or low-resolution, and where aggregating recognition results along a text trajectory leads to far more reliable spotting.

The work is a collaboration between **RIKEN**, **Hanoi University of Science and Technology**, **Nagoya University**, **Lawrence Technological University**, and **Ritsumeikan University**.
