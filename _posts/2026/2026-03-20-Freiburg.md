I had the pleasure of attending the **Freiburg Rising Stars Conference 2025/2026**, hosted in **Freiburg, Germany** — a unique event bringing together outstanding early-career researchers from diverse disciplines. The conference provided an inspiring platform to exchange ideas, present research, and connect with peers and senior academics in a highly collaborative environment.

---

## 🌟 Highlights

Throughout the event, I had the opportunity to:

- Engage in insightful discussions across interdisciplinary topics  
- Learn from keynote talks and panel sessions led by leading researchers  
- Present my work and receive valuable feedback  
- Connect with fellow rising researchers from around the world
