I am deeply honored to share that our paper received the **Best Oral Award** at the **7th International Conference on ACM Multimedia Asia 2025 (MMAsia 2025)**, held in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

This recognition means a lot to our team and marks a memorable milestone in my research journey.

---

## 🏆 Best Oral Award

According to the official certificate, the award was presented for our paper:

> **"Q-Adapter: Visual Query Adapter for Extracting Textually-related Features in Video Captioning."**
> Authors: Junan Chen, Trung Thanh Nguyen, Takahiro Komamizu, Ichiro Ide  

Receiving this award during the conference was both surprising and incredibly motivating. 
It reflects not only the technical contribution of our work but also the clarity and impact of our presentation.
