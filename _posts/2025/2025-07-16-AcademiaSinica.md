This summer, I had the wonderful opportunity to attend the **International Summer School 2025** organized by the **Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica**, in Taipei, Taiwan.

The program took place from **June 25 to July 16, 2025**, and focused on:

> **Nonstationary Time Series Analysis for Biomedical Artificial Intelligence**

It was an intensive and intellectually rewarding three-week experience that combined advanced mathematical theory with modern AI applications in biomedical research.

---

## 🎓 Certificate of Achievement

At the end of the program, I received a **Certificate of Achievement** from Academia Sinica, recognizing the successful completion of the course. This certification represents not only participation, but also deep engagement with advanced topics in mathematical modeling and biomedical AI.
