I had the honor of attending the IEEE International Conference on Automatic Face and Gesture Recognition (FG 2024) to present my research on "One-Stage Open-Vocabulary Temporal Action Detection Leveraging Temporal Multi-scale and Action Label Features." The conference was an amazing experience, filled with insightful discussions, exciting presentations, and great networking opportunities.

<div style="text-align: center;">
    {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/2024/fg-01.jpg" width="70%" %}
</div>

In my talk, I introduced a novel one-stage open-vocabulary approach for temporal action detection, which leverages both multi-scale temporal features and action label embeddings. This method aims to improve action recognition in real-world, open-vocabulary settings, addressing the challenge of detecting actions beyond predefined categories.
