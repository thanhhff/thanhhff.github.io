I had the incredible opportunity to present my latest research at ACM Multimedia Asia 2024, held from December 3–6, 2024, in Auckland, New Zealand. The conference brought together researchers and practitioners from the multimedia community to share cutting-edge advancements and insights.

<div style="text-align: center;">
    {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/2024/mmasia-3.jpg" width="70%" %}

During the conference, I presented my paper titled "Action Selection Learning for Multi-label Multi-view Action Recognition". My research focuses on enhancing action recognition models by leveraging multi-view perspectives and multi-label learning techniques to improve accuracy and robustness in real-world scenarios.
