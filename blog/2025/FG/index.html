🇺🇸 Best Student Paper Award at IEEE FG 2025

Created in May 20, 2025

2025   ·   fg   us   ·   conference

I am deeply honored to share that our paper received the Best Student Paper Award at the IEEE International Conference on Automatic Face and Gesture Recognition (FG 2025).

This recognition is a significant milestone in my PhD journey and a proud moment for our entire research team.

🏆 Awarded Paper

The award was presented at FG 2025, held in Clearwater, Florida, USA. Our awarded paper is titled:

MultiSensor-Home: A Wide-area Multi-modal Multi-view Dataset for Action Recognition and Transformer-based Sensor Fusion

Authors: Trung Thanh Nguyen, Yasutomo Kawanishi, Vijay John, Takahiro Komamizu, and Ichiro Ide

Receiving this award among many strong international submissions was both surprising and incredibly motivating.

🌊 Conference Experience in Florida

FG 2025 was held in Clearwater, Florida — a beautiful coastal city.

🤝 Gratitude

I would like to sincerely thank my co-authors and mentors for their continuous guidance and support. This achievement would not have been possible without their collaboration and encouragement.

Winning the Best Student Paper Award at IEEE FG 2025 strengthens my motivation to continue pursuing impactful research.
